Privacy Policy

How TumaiLife Technologies Co. collects, protects, and respects your personal data.

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

At TumaiLife Technologies Co. ("we," "our," or "us"), we value your trust and are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle personal information gathered through our official website and client communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting project consultation forms, newsletters, or direct email inquiries:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, and phone number.
  • Project Briefs: Details regarding your software, UI/UX, SEO, or site maintenance needs.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymous session logs, browser type, and page interaction data to ensure website security and optimal load performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used strictly for technical operations and direct communication:

  • Responding to your project requests and consultation messages.
  • Structuring service scope proposals, custom software plans, and maintenance agreements.
  • Improving site load speed, user interface design, and overall digital platform security.

3. Data Protection and Storage

We implement modern encryption standards, secure database row policies, and server safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. We never sell, lease, or share your contact details with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our site uses minimal session cookies to remember browser settings, optimize page assets, and monitor uptime. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though some functional elements may render differently.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for your contact details to be deleted from our inquiry records at any time.

6. Contacting Our Data Lead

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please get in touch with us directly at info@tumailife.com.
